# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want Storage Sweep to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity.

**Please do:**

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Assume good intent and give constructive feedback.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was a first-time contributor once.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

**Please don't:**

- Harass, insult or demean anyone.
- Use sexualized language or imagery.
- Publish anyone's private information without permission.
- Engage in trolling or personal or political attacks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider harmful.

To report a problem privately, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/TNH-Labs-LK/StorageSweep/security/advisories/new) or message the maintainers through the [TNH-Labs-LK](https://github.com/TNH-Labs-LK) GitHub profile.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including its enforcement guidelines, applies to this project.
