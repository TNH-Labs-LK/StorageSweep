## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added

- Overview with disk usage, scan totals and Trash size.
- Caches & Junk: app caches, logs, developer caches, Xcode, Android, AI models, device backups.
- Project Dependencies: `node_modules`, Python environments, build folders.
- Large Files, Installers, Duplicates (SHA-256), Applications (with leftover app data) and Space Explorer.
- Move to Trash with a confirmation list, and Empty Trash.
- Protected-path safety rules.
- Full Disk Access guide.
